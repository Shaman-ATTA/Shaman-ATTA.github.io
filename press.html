Block Fill: One Line Puzzle

Short description

Draw one line. Fill every block. A calm one-line puzzle with 2,000 levels that plays fully offline.

Description

Block Fill is a calm, clever one-line puzzle: draw a single path that fills every block on the board. No timers, no pressure — just you, a grid and one continuous line.

Levels start small so you learn fast, then the grid grows — with more walls and tricky branches. A fresh daily puzzle keeps a streak going, and the game works fully offline, on a plane or on the couch. Hints show the next part of the path, never the whole solution.

Chroma Shift

Short description

Tap cells, shift colors, clear the board. A calm color puzzle with 40 handcrafted levels.

Description

Chroma Shift is a colorful logic puzzle from ATTA CORP. Tap any cell to cycle its color — and its neighbors. Match every cell to the target shade to clear the level.

Forty handcrafted levels, undo and reset, and optional video hints. English and Russian are built in — pick one in Settings. Ads are served by Google AdMob; you can remove them anytime with a one-time purchase through Google Play. No account required.

Rustroot: Colony Survival

Short description

Lead survivors after the collapse: scavenge, farm, build and hold off raiders.

Description

Rustroot is a top-down colony survival sim built for phones. Colonists eat, sleep, get tired, make friends and break down under stress. Scavenge ruins, farm, build walls, workshops and turrets, research three eras of tech, defend against raiders and harsh winters, and send caravans across the world map. Plays fully offline.

Crownless: Medieval Kingdoms

Short description

Raise an army, fight lords and bandits, and win back your family’s lost crown.

Description

Crownless is a medieval sandbox strategy RPG for phones. Ride across a living 3D world of five kingdoms, 74 towns, villages and castles and 15 lords with their own plans. Recruit infantry, archers and cavalry, fight real-time battles, besiege castles, trade and take on quests. Plays fully offline, no ads.
