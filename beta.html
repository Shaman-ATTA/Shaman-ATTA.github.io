Testers wanted

Play Chroma Shift before everyone else

Chroma Shift is in closed testing on Google Play. Join the beta, play for free, and help us shape the final game. Please keep it installed for at least 14 days — that helps us launch.

  1. Join the testers group

    Open the Google Group with the Google account you use on your phone and press “Join group”.

    Open the group

  2. Become a tester & install

    Open the testing link, tap “Become a tester”, then install Chroma Shift from Google Play.

    Become a tester

Why test? You get the game first, your ideas can land in the release, and you help an indie studio get onto Google Play. Feedback: gamerazrabplay@gmail.com
