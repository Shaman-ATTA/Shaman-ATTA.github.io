About the game

Crownless is a strategy adventure from ATTA CORP. Five winters ago a usurper betrayed your father, seized your lands and hid the ancient crown behind the walls of Castle Veir. You are a nameless exile — until you raise an army and take it back.

Ride a living realm, forge alliances, manage your warband and fight real-time battles you can command by hand or trust to auto-orders. The world and its kingdoms are much larger than a single campaign map. Crownless is in active development and is not yet on Google Play.

Become a tester

This game is in free closed testing on Google Play. It takes about a minute:

  1. Join the testers group

    Open the Google Group with the Google account you use on your phone and press “Join group”. One group covers all our test games.

    Open the group

  2. Opt in on Google Play

    Open the game’s testing link with the same account and tap “Become a tester”.

    Become a tester

  3. Install and keep it

    After you opt in, the testing page shows a “Download it on Google Play” link (it can take a few minutes to appear). Install, play a bit and keep the game installed for 14 days.

Why test? You get the game first, your ideas can land in the release, and you help an indie studio get onto Google Play. Found a bug? Write to gamerazrabplay@gmail.com with your phone model and Android version.
