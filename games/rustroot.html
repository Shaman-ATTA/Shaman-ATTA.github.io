About the game

Rustroot is a mobile-first colony survival sim set after the collapse. You guide a handful of survivors who salvage ruins, farm hardy tubers, build shelter and hold off raiders — and you watch their story unfold.

The world is procedural: day and night, raids that scale with your wealth, a wider map with caravans, and colonists with needs, moods and relationships. It is built for touch and shorter sessions. Rustroot is in active development and is not yet on Google Play.
