About the studio

We make games that respect your time

ATTA CORP is an independent studio. We design, build and polish original mobile games — casual puzzles you can finish on a commute, and deeper worlds you can sink into for weeks. A small team plays every game every day.

Easy to start

No tutorial walls, no sign-ups. Open the game and play within seconds.

Built to last

We polish systems until they stay fun — offline when we can, honest about ads and purchases.

Built with players

We test early with our community and ship updates based on your feedback.
