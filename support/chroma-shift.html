Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Tap a cell to cycle its color — and its neighbors. Make every cell match the target shade to clear the level. Undo and reset are always available.

How do I join the beta?

Join our Google Group, then open the testing link on the beta page with the same account and tap “Become a tester”.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings in the game and pick English or Русский.

How do I remove ads?

Use “Remove Ads” in the game — a one-time purchase through Google Play. Tips to the studio are optional.

How do I get a refund?

All purchases are processed by Google Play. You can request a refund from your Google Play order history. If something went wrong, email us too.

What data do you keep? How do I delete it?

We don’t run accounts or keep your personal data on our servers. Progress is stored on your phone; uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it. Ads and payments are handled by Google — see the privacy policy for details.

How do I leave the beta?

Open the same testing link and choose “Leave the program”. You can then reinstall the public version when the game launches.

How do I report a bug?

Email gamerazrabplay@gmail.com with a short description, your phone model, Android version and, if possible, a screenshot.

Still need help?

Write to us — we read every message. Please add your phone model, Android version and the game version (Settings or the Google Play page).
