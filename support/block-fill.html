Frequently asked questions

Does the game work offline?

Yes. Block Fill plays fully offline. An internet connection is only needed for ads and purchases.

How do I remove ads?

Open Settings and tap “Remove Ads” — it is a one-time purchase through Google Play. If the button says “Loading…” or “Unavailable”, check your internet connection and Google Play sign-in, then try again.

I bought Remove Ads but still see ads

Make sure the phone is signed in to the same Google account you used to buy, then open the game with internet on — it re-checks your purchases with Google Play automatically.

Will my progress carry over to a new phone?

Progress is saved on your device only, so a new phone starts from level 1. Your Remove Ads purchase stays with your Google account.

How do hints work?

A hint shows the next part of the path, never the whole solution. You get free hints, and you can watch an optional short video for another.

How do I get a refund?

All purchases are processed by Google Play. You can request a refund from your Google Play order history. If something went wrong, email us too.

What data do you keep? How do I delete it?

We don’t run accounts or keep your personal data on our servers. Progress is stored on your phone; uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it. Ads and payments are handled by Google — see the privacy policy for details.

How do I report a bug?

Email gamerazrabplay@gmail.com with a short description, your phone model, Android version and, if possible, a screenshot.

Still need help?

Write to us — we read every message. Please add your phone model, Android version and the game version (Settings or the Google Play page).
