What's inside

  • Offline first-aid & survival guideStep-by-step guides based on official public sources (Ready.gov, FEMA, CDC). Works with no connection.
  • Emergency numbers by countryThe right numbers for where you are, one tap to dial.
  • SOS to people nearbySend an SOS — with your location if you want — and choose who gets it: everyone nearby, your group or one person.
  • Nearby chat with no internetPhones connect over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi and relay messages to each other. No SIM, no network, no extra hardware.
  • End-to-end encrypted private messagesOnly the recipient can read your direct messages. Compare a safety code in person.
  • Encrypted groups via QRFor teachers and families: create a group, others join by scanning a QR code.
  • 6 languagesEnglish, Русский, עברית, Español, Deutsch, Français.
  • No ads, no payments, no accountFree. Nothing to buy, nothing to sign up for. Your data stays on your phone.
