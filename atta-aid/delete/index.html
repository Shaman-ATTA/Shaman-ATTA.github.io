ATTA Aid — Delete your data

App: ATTA Aid (Android, package com.attacorp.aid) · Developer: ATTA CORP (Google Play developer “ATTA”) · Contact: gamerazrabplay@gmail.com · Last updated: 16 November 2026

Short version

ATTA Aid has no accounts and no servers. Everything the app stores is kept only on your phone, so you can delete it yourself at any time — you don't need to ask us.

How to delete your data

  1. Message history only: in the app open Chat → Messenger settings → Clear message history.
  2. Leave a group (deletes the group and its key from your phone): Chat → Messenger settings → My group → Leave group.
  3. Everything at once: Android Settings → Apps → ATTA Aid → Storage → Clear data (on some phones “Clear storage” or “Manage storage”).
  4. Uninstall ATTA Aid — this also deletes all of its data from the phone.

What gets deleted

Options 3 and 4 delete everything the app stores on your phone:

  • your chat nickname, the random device ID and your chat encryption keys (and the public keys of people you chatted with);
  • your message history;
  • groups and group keys;
  • blocked users and reports;
  • your age group and the parent PIN;
  • learning progress, quiz results, go-bag checklist and settings.

What we keep

  • The developer keeps nothing. We have no servers and receive no data from the app.
  • Messages you already sent are stored on the phones of the people who received them. Only they can delete them on their devices.
  • Learning progress and settings may be included in your own Android backup in your Google account (chat data, keys, age group and PIN are excluded from backups). That backup follows your Google account settings; you can delete it in Google Drive / Android backup settings.
  • If you e-mailed us (for example a report or feedback at gamerazrabplay@gmail.com), you can ask us to delete that correspondence. We do so within 30 days.
